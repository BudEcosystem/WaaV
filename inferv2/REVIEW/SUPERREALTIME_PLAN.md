## Thesis
WaaV already OUT-DESIGNS vLLM-Omni in machinery but loses on DELIVERY: the recurring failure across all 9 gaps is the design-vs-wired trap (decode_committed_prefix seam wired but zero prod model overrides — CONFIRMED only arstep.rs:615 default + serve.rs:3416 test-double; StepBuckets/CascadeDag/LayeredAdmit/KvFirewall built+byte-tested with zero live serve-path callers; SDP AttnKernel::{Flash,Cudnn} collapse to FusedAuto). To BEAT vLLM-Omni we do NOT chase attention kernels (measured 1.5-5% of every model — a <1% net lever by ridge-433.5 physics) or fabricate throughput. We attack the ON-BOX MEASURED truth: B=1 decode is launch/CPU-bound (dia2 gpu_busy 0.06, ~380K tiny launches, ~12.3s GPU-idle), and TTFA == whole-utterance is the #1 realized-latency gap. The winning spine is four ordered moves, each byte-identical-by-default or an explicit WER/MOS-gated opt-in: (1) a WIRED-LEDGER discipline that makes dormancy a CI red-bar so every capability proves a LIVE serve-path caller; (2) kill the 12 per-frame GPU->CPU sync round-trips + stream first-audio early + extend byte-identical CUDA-graph coverage into the batched path — the launch/sync/latency levers that actually move GB10; (3) in-process multi-stage DAG (beats their 22GB per-process bug) + byte-identical cross-request flow batching + measure-gated width, all labeled THROUGHPUT/capacity not single-stream RTF and gated on measured fill; (4) quant/hardware coverage as an honest COVERAGE win with explicit projection-only exclusions. Governing law throughout: the invariant hierarchy byte-identity > state-safety > lockstep > OOM-floor > perf-levers; any lever that would violate a higher tier is refused at construction or routed to a stamped WER/MOS path, never silently. Every projection is re-derived from profiling/perf_equations.py against gb10_calibration.json (198.5 GB/s, 86.04 TFLOP/s, ridge 433.5) — and the datasheet 273e9 constant that poisons every duty projection (LIVE at engine.rs:1889) is corrected in Wave 0.
